## About

I design and ship automation for businesses: CRM systems, sales funnels, and AI voice agents that qualify leads and book appointments without manual follow-up. My work connects tools through REST APIs and webhooks so that a lead coming in on a landing page ends up in the right pipeline, gets the right message, and lands on a calendar.

Behind that sits a computer science degree with a data science major, and a research background in machine learning, deep learning and computer vision. Based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
